Topic 3 · Recap

Saying no

With be: add not

I am not tired.

She isn’t tired.

They aren’t tired.

Men charchagan emasman.

With actions: don’t / doesn’t

I don’t like fish.

He doesn’t like fish.

We don’t like fish.

Men baliqni yoqtirmayman.

Topic 3 · Recap

Topic 6 · Recap

Have to: four forms

I · you · we · they

I have to work.

Men ishlashim kerak.

he · she · it

She has to work.

U ishlashi kerak.

Negative

He doesn’t have to work.

U ishlashi shart emas.

Question

Do you have to work?

Ishlashingiz kerakmi?

Topic 6 · Recap

Must or have to?

must

My own rule, I feel it is important

I must study harder.

have to

A rule from outside: school, job, law

I have to wear a uniform.

mustn’t = forbidden

You mustn’t smoke here.

Bu yerda chekish mumkin emas.

don’t have to = not needed

You don’t have to pay.

Pul to’lashingiz shart emas.
